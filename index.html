Leadership Philosophy

How I Lead

Enable. Don't Just Manage.

The best outcomes come not from control but from creating the conditions for talented people to operate without unnecessary friction. My job is to clear the path, not stand in the middle of it.

Systems Thinking Over Siloed Expertise.

AI transformation fails most often not because of technical gaps, but because functions that need to work together don't. I build bridges across data, IT, operations, and executive leadership.

Evidence as the Basis for Trust.

In high-stakes transformation environments, trust is earned through rigour. I prioritize data-informed decisions, transparent prioritization, and frameworks that give stakeholders confidence to move forward.

Servant Leadership.

I am here to amplify the people around me. That means coaching, removing blockers, and building the organizational conditions that allow teams to grow into their best work — not performing leadership.

The Connection

Why the University of Guelph

This is not a transition. It is a convergence. The AVP of AI role asks for someone who can translate enterprise AI capability into institutional strategy — who understands governance not as a constraint but as a design principle — and who brings the cross-functional credibility to align faculty, researchers, administrators, and students around a shared framework for responsible innovation.

That is precisely the profile twelve years at Salesforce produced. And the University of Guelph is where my own foundation was built.

Universities are at a genuine inflection point in the AI evolution. The question is not whether AI will reshape research, teaching, and operations. It already is. The question is whether the institution shapes that transformation deliberately — with values intact — or responds to it reactively. The AVP of AI is the person who makes the difference between those two outcomes.
